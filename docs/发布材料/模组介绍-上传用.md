## Description（完整介绍 — 英文在上，中文在下）

```
### Homestead Sell Veggies
A Forge 1.20.1 homestead mod that turns surplus crops and goods into a **net shop loop**: list stock from your chests, browse a themed mall, pay from a **wallet balance**, and receive **gravity parcel boxes** stacked at harvest points.
### Features
**Open the shop**
- Default key **H** (rebindable). Tabs: **Mall**, **My Shop**, **Logistics**, **Harvest Points**.
**Sell (My Shop)**
- Use a **Source Compass** to mark chests as stock sources. List items with quantity (max 64) and your own unit price; see the day's suggested price band.
- Relative to the live market reference: underpriced listings can sell quickly; overpriced ones take longer; at **≥2.5×** reference they stay listed but never sell (cancel anytime).
- Shop levels **0–5** raise listing slots (10 → 60) from cumulative **sell revenue** only.
**Buy (Mall)**
- Many preset shops (produce, ore, food, building, florist, and more). Search, filter by shop, daily shuffled shelves, **hot** and **sale** badges.
- **Cart** checkout or buy now; sale items capped at **64 per in-game day**.
- Product pages show star reviews in a casual buyer voice.
**Wallet & logistics**
- Balance is a per-player account with a bill ledger (click **Balance** in the UI).
- After checkout, parcels go **in transit** with a random courier (fast / mid / slow). Track each shipment on the Logistics tab.
- Place **Harvest Points**, pick a default drop-off, and parcels fall like sand into stacked **Parcel Boxes**; right-click to open.
**Market**
- Catalog + recipe pricing; daily reference bands move with your buy/sell volume. Vanilla crops, ores, farm goods, and many mod foods are supported when present.
### Optional integrations
| Mod | Notes |
|---|---|
| Farmer's Delight | Rice, crates, crops, milk bottle, etc. |
| Youkai's Homecoming | Tea, pods, machine-crafted foods priced from ingredients |
| Delightful | Cantaloupe and related items |
No hard dependency—missing mods simply omit their items.
### Commands
- `/sellveg balance <player> <amount>` — set wallet balance (op)
### Version
- Minecraft **1.20.1** · Loader: **Forge**
### Config
`config/homestead_sell_veg-common.toml` — buy multiplier, market bands, listing delays, shop upgrade thresholds, delivery timing, sale daily cap, and more. Rejoin after edits.
---
### 多多卖菜
**Homestead Sell Veggies**
Forge 1.20.1 生存轻经营模组：把箱子里堆不下的农产与杂物挂上网店卖掉，再在主题商城里购物车网购；用**账户余额**结算，**重力快递盒**会叠落在你的**收货点**上。
### 主要功能
**打开网店**
- 默认按 **H**（可改键）。含 **商城**、**我的店铺**、**物流**、**收货点** 四个页签。
**卖（我的店铺）**
- **货源指南针**右键标记箱子为货源，在上架面板选库存、填数量（最多 64）与单价，旁侧显示当日建议价区间。
- 相对当日市价：低价可快售；高价等待更久；**≥2.5 倍**参考价可挂单但永不成交，随时下架退回货源箱（装不下或区块未加载则进背包）。
- 店铺 **0–5 级**随累计**卖出成交额**升级，挂单槽 **10 → 60**（网购消费不计入）。
**买（商城）**
- 多家预设店铺（农产、矿石、食品、建材、花店等）；支持搜索、按店筛选、每日固定打乱货架，以及**热销**与**促销**角标。
- **购物车**结算或立即购买；促销商品每日限购 **64** 个。
- 商品详情含星级评价，评语口吻像买家随手写的。
**余额与物流**
- 余额为玩家账户，网店内点**余额**可查看账单。
- 下单后包裹**在途**，随机快递公司（快 / 中 / 中偏慢）；物流页逐单显示名称、数量、状态与预计送达。
- 放置**收货点**并设默认投递点；**快递盒**像沙子一样落下叠放，右键拆开取货。
**市场**
- 目录价 + 配方价定价，参考价带随当日买卖量浮动；支持原版农矿食杂及已安装模组中的对应物品。
### 可选联动
| 模组 | 说明 |
|---|---|
| 农夫乐事 | 稻米、箱装作物、奶瓶等 |
| 妖怪的归家 | 茶叶、豆荚及按原料计价的料理 |
| Delightful | 甜瓜及相关物品 |
均为软联动，未安装则不上架对应物品。
### 指令
- `/sellveg balance <玩家> <数额>` — 设置余额（需 OP）
### 版本
- Minecraft **1.20.1** · 加载器：**Forge**
### 配置
`config/homestead_sell_veg-common.toml` — 买入倍率、市价带、挂单延迟、升级门槛、送达时间、促销日限等；改后重进世界生效。
```
