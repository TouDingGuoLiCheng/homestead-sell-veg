English prompt
Short game SFX of opening a cardboard parcel: a sharp adhesive-tape rip, then a corrugated box flap popping open with dry cardboard rustle, ending with a tiny contents clink. Close-mic, dry, snappy UI feedback, no music, no voice, no long reverb. 0.4–0.5 seconds, mono.
